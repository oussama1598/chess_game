# Salon textures

These textures are derived from CC0 assets published by Poly Haven:

- **Wooden Chair 01**, Jake Mobley:
  https://polyhaven.com/a/WoodenChair_01
- **Fancy Picture Frame 01**, Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers:
  https://polyhaven.com/a/fancy_picture_frame_01
- **Herringbone Parquet**, Jenelle van Heerden and Sergej Majboroda:
  https://polyhaven.com/a/herringbone_parquet

The 1K diffuse maps are redistributed unchanged. Files ending in `_spec_1k`
were generated locally by inverting the source roughness channel for the
renderer's two-texture material workflow. The original ARM/roughness source
maps are retained beside the derived maps for provenance and reproducibility.

All Poly Haven assets are released under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://polyhaven.com/license
